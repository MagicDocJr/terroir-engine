### Simulation and science (for the science review)
- Côte d'Or spring frost is too frequent for early-budding vines (seen in Tasting-practice pours 2026-09-29, cotedor-village): Chardonnay loses 82 % in 2019, 29 % in 2020 and 95 % in 2022, Pinot Noir 54 % in 2022, while real 2019/2020/2022 losses were local and small; 2016 and 2021 are right. Check budburst timing against the frost threshold and the grid-to-block night cooling (KPocket) before the next calibration pass. Wine Fair stands avoid those years until then.
- Mosel 2010 is not reproduced: the sim gives a normal crop (≈ 69 hL/ha) and ordinary acidity, while the real vintage had a tiny crop from poor flowering and very high acidity (docs/science/vintage-notes.md). Fruit set in a cold, wet June needs a look; the Wine Fair's 2010 stand was dropped until then.
- Côte d'Or 2003 is picked mid-September in the sim; the real harvest started in August. Part of the known small ripening spread between years.
- Found building the Cause catalogue (2026-10-02): Pinot Noir never reads "pale" on the Tasting sheet (the colour cut-offs need the calibration the sheet doc already flags); Mosel Auslese never gets noble rot while late-picked Côte d'Or Pinot Noir does (and noble rot on red grapes is a defect, not a style: check the botrytis model and the sheet's family); the rustic cellar style practically never develops a fault (VA, Brett, H₂S, oxidation), so fault spotting has no practice pours yet. These Causes are reviewed by chain questions until fixed (`reviewBy: chain`), and `pinot-pale-colour` waits outside the catalogue.
- The Tasting sheet's quality verdict skews high (most simulated Wines come out Very good or Outstanding, seen in Tasting-practice pours 2026-09-29); for the Conclusions exercise the spread needs calibrating with the WSET L3 reviewer.
- Côte d'Or ripening spread between years is too small: 2003 reaches 200 g/L on 4 Sep in the game, the real harvest began ≈ 19 Aug. Diagnosed in `phenology.md` §9: GFV flowering responds too weakly to warm springs (observed 1991–2003 flowering spread 25 days, model 14) and the post-véraison drought brake slows the hot, dry years. Needs a yearly Côte de Beaune flowering/véraison series to refit, and 2003 Ψstem data. Tools: `sim ripecheck`, `sim frostcheck`, `sim yieldcheck`.
- Napa year-to-year yield variation is 4–7 %; check against the Napa County crush reports.
- Multi-vintage carry-over coefficients (fruitfulness, reserves, inoculum, 20 % leaf-loss tolerance) are `UNVERIFIED`.
- Reserves ignore frost at leaf fall (spec §4.2 lists it).
- The Guyot cap is now 24 buds per vine (a double Guyot, 2026-10-10), so Carneros' Balanced crop rose to 68 hL/ha (≈ 9.4 t/ha Pinot Noir): check against the Napa County crush reports for Carneros Pinot Noir. (The 8-bud Village/1er Cru limit is warned at pruning and planting since 2026-10-11; the Wine is declassified at bottling, as the rules do. A Block planted at 9,000 vines/ha breaks it with a Standard pruning: the Planting screen says so.)
- Napa Bench Cordon Blocks get 8–12 two-bud spurs per vine (16–24 buds), while the §3.1 bud table says 6–10 spurs × 2 buds; check whether the table means per arm (bilateral cordon) and align the table or the bud-load conversion.
