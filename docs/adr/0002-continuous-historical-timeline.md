# Continuous historical Timeline instead of selected vintages

A game runs on one continuous calendar. It starts in the Planting year the player picks (1990–2022), uses real archived daily weather for every year the archive covers, and switches to seeded Synthetic weather (recent climate plus the warming trend) after that. We rejected a small set of hand-picked "easy / hard / extreme" vintages because the continuous Timeline teaches real vintage variation and climate change as lived experience, and leads naturally into multi-vintage play (the first expansion).

## Consequences
- The MVP plays only one Vintage, but all state (vines, cash, stock, staff) must be built to carry over between years.
- Staff and forecasts must never read the real future weather that is stored in the data. Advice is limited to what was knowable on that day.
- Two anachronisms are accepted and labelled as game simplifications: EUR at 2026 price levels in every year, and today's wine law in every year.
